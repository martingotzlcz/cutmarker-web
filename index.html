FAQ

Good questions

Which Premiere version do I need?

Adobe Premiere Pro 2020 or newer. It installs as a panel under Window, then Extensions.

Does it work in DaVinci Resolve?

Premiere Pro today. DaVinci support is on the roadmap for Auto Multicam, Silence Cut, and Final Check. Mic Bleed stays Premiere only, because DaVinci handles track audio differently.

Do I need a mic for each speaker?

For multicam, yes. Cutmarker decides who is on camera by listening to each mic, so one mic per speaker gives the cleanest result.

Is my footage uploaded anywhere?

Never. Your media and editing stay on your computer. No uploads, no per-minute fees, no monthly limits.

Do I need an internet connection?

Your media and the editing stay on your computer, nothing is uploaded. You do need a connection to activate and verify your license, the same as most paid editing software. The editing itself runs locally.

Do I need Mic Bleed if my mixer already has automix?

Even when your mixer or recorder reduces bleed while you record, that pass is not always perfect. You can still run Mic Bleed in post to fine-tune it further, with keyframes you control, whenever you want a cleaner result.

What does Insights actually track?

Only your work inside Premiere: Editing time and actions on the timeline, as an orientational guide. It does not watch anything you do outside the app, and nothing leaves your computer.

How many devices does one license cover?

One device per license. Changing computers? Just reach out to us and we will move your license to the new machine.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. It is a monthly subscription with no lock-in, cancel whenever you want. The 14-day trial is free, and you are not charged if you cancel before it ends.
