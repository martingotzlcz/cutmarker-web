FAQ

Good questions.

Which Premiere version do I need?

Adobe Premiere Pro 2023 or newer. Cutmarker installs as a panel under Window → Extensions.

Does it work with just one microphone?

Yes. Unlike most tools, Cutmarker works from a single mic all the way up to ten — so even simpler setups get a clean multicam edit.

Is my footage uploaded to the cloud?

Never. Everything runs locally on your machine. No uploads, no per-minute fees, no monthly processing limits.

What makes Mic Bleed different from a VST?

A VST is a black box you can't touch. Mic Bleed writes real volume keyframes straight into your timeline, so you keep full control and can fine-tune any moment by hand.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes — 30-day free trial, cancel anytime, no questions asked.
